# Wallpaper credits

All images below are authentic Brave New Tab wallpapers contributed by the listed photographers/creators. They come from `brave/brave-core` at the pinned commits noted here and from Brave’s live NTP Background Images component (v1.0.25). No sponsored images, no New Tab Takeover ads, no stock-photo API. Brave’s own attribution data (`backgrounds.ts` / `photo.json`) was used for the credits. Images were resized to JPEG for cross-browser support.
